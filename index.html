Build in Congo with Confidence

Living abroad should not prevent you from building at home. Congo Connect is a proposed service that helps diaspora clients plan construction, verify property details, and follow project progress in Lubumbashi and Kinshasa.

OUR PURPOSE

Clear information. Better decisions.

Building from another country can create uncertainty about costs, timelines, materials, and site activity. Our concept focuses on transparent communication and practical oversight throughout the project journey.

01

Plan

Clarify your goals, preferred location, budget, and project scope before making commitments.

02

Verify

Coordinate property information checks and document review with qualified local professionals.

03

Track

Use milestones, budget summaries, and visual reports to understand how work is progressing.
