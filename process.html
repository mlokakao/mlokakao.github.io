How It Works

A straightforward five-step journey designed to help clients stay informed while they are living abroad.

Five steps from idea to handover

  1. Initial consultation: Identify the type of project, city, intended use, estimated budget, and timeline.
  2. Verification and planning: Identify documentation needs, discuss feasibility, and develop an initial scope and schedule.
  3. Coordination: Align project participants on responsibilities, work phases, materials, and expected updates.
  4. Progress reporting: Share photo or video summaries, milestone status, and budget notes for review.
  5. Completion and handover: Review the work against the agreed scope and document outstanding items.
