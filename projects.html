Projects & Planning

Every project is different. The examples below illustrate how we would organize the scope and progress reporting for clients.

Example project types

These are illustrative scenarios, not completed Congo Connect client projects. They show how a remote client could define milestones and information needs before work begins.

Illustrative project planning comparison
Project TypePrimary FocusExample Progress UpdatesClient Decision
Family HomeResidential constructionFoundation, walls, roofing, finishingApprove scope and milestone budget

RenovationExisting building improvementsBefore-and-after photos, materials, inspection notesPrioritize repairs and change requests

Why transparency matters

For clients living outside the DRC, regular status reporting helps reveal potential delays and unresolved questions early. The goal is to support informed decisions rather than promise that every construction risk can be eliminated.
